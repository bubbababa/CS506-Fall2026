About This Demonstration

This tool demonstrates the "multiple comparisons problem" - when you test many variables, you're likely to find at least one "significant" result by chance alone. Enter data for 5 students before and after a study, then see how many variables show "significant" changes!

💡 What This Means

With 6 statistical tests, there's about a 26% chance of finding at least one "significant" result purely by chance (even if nothing actually changed). This is the multiple comparisons problem!

Key Takeaway: When you test many variables, you need to adjust your significance level or use methods like Bonferroni correction to avoid false positives.

About This Experiment

This experiment demonstrates confirmation bias - the tendency to search for, interpret, and recall information in a way that confirms one's preexisting beliefs.

The actual rule is simple: a < b < c (the numbers must be in ascending order)

Notice how your choice of examples might be influenced by your hypothesized rule, leading you to test examples that confirm rather than challenge your hypothesis.

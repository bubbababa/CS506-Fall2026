Larger σ² widens the vertical spread around the line; larger n fills the histogram more smoothly and makes β̂ less variable across resamples (try “Resample” a few times). The purple curve uses your chosen population σ², not the RMSE from the single fit—so with small n the histogram can look rough even when the model is correct.
